Dear Students,

I am extremely happy to announce the launch of "Design Your Degree" program under the Skill, Incubation, Innovation, and Entrepreneurial Development Centre. As the Director of this initiative, it is my honor to introduce you to this innovative 4-year undergraduate degree program.

At University of Jammu, we have always been at the forefront of pioneering education, and this new program is a testament to our commitment to empowering students to shape their academic journey. The "Design Your Degree" program is a unique opportunity for each student to personalize their learning experience, aligning it precisely with their individual passions, aspirations, and career goals.

This program is a bold step towards revolutionizing traditional education, and I am confident that it will empower each student to become a trailblazer in their respective fields. We believe that by providing you with the tools and resources to design your academic journey, we are fostering a new generation of innovators and leaders who will make a significant impact on society.

I encourage all students to embrace this opportunity with an open mind and a passion for exploration. Let us embark on this exciting journey together, and I am eager to witness the incredible achievements that will undoubtedly arise from the "Design Your Degree" program.

Prof. Alka Sharma

Director
