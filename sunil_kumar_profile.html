Sunil Kumar

SUNIL KUMAR, ASSISTANT PROFESSOR

Department of Statistics

Hello All, I did Ph.D. in Statistics (Title: "Improved Estimation of Population Parameters in Presence of Non-response"), M.Phil in Statistics (Title: "Estimation of Population Mean Using Auxiliary Variable in Presence of Non-response in Sample Surveys") and M.Sc. in Statistics. I am currently working as Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics, University of Jammu. Previously, I worked for around four years (June 2013 – June 2017) at Alliance University, Bangalore, India. Before that, I worked as Visiting Scientist/Faculty at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, India, and as Visiting Faculty at IIM, Indore. My research areas include Sampling Theory, Estimation Theory, and Latent Class Analysis for identifying groups of people giving extreme responses in surveys, along with Statistical Consultancy.

  • Published more than 90 international research papers in Indexed/SCOPUS/SCI journals.
  • Regular Referee of more than 20 statistical journals.
  • Board Member of the "Journal of Advanced Computing".
  • Editorial Board Member of the "International Journal of Business Management and Economics Research (IJBMER)".
