Dr. Sandeep Arya

DR. SANDEEP ARYA, ASSOCIATE PROFESSOR

Department of Physics

Sandeep Arya received his PhD in Physics from the University of Jammu (India). He is currently working as Senior Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics, University of Jammu. His research interests include synthesis and characterization of nanomaterials for sensing, energy storage, and energy harvesting applications. He is the recipient of several national fellowships and has published more than 70 research papers in various journals of repute.
