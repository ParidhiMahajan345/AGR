🎯 About the Programme

Design Your Degree is a flagship programme of the University of Jammu under NEP-2020, representing a transformative revolution in higher education. This innovative four-year programme is designed specifically for students seeking to harness the power of personalized, interdisciplinary education tailored to their unique aspirations and career objectives.

With the transformative motto of "Teach to Transform, Educate to Empower and Learn to Lead," this programme equips students with the agility, critical thinking capabilities, and diverse skill set required to excel in the dynamic and rapidly evolving professional landscape of tomorrow.

Unlike traditional one-size-fits-all degree programmes, Design Your Degree empowers learners to curate their own personalized curriculum by strategically selecting courses across diverse disciplines. This innovative approach effectively breaks down academic silos and fosters genuine interdisciplinary learning, preparing students with not just specialized domain knowledge, but with the unprecedented versatility and adaptability demanded by the modern workforce.

The programme recognizes that today's graduates must possess not merely specialized knowledge, but a comprehensive and diverse skill set. With the swift pace of technological advancement, the modern economy demands flexible professionals capable of seamlessly transitioning between various fields and navigating complex, interconnected challenges.
