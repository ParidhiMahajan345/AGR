Design Your Degree

Design Your Degree is a pioneering interdisciplinary programme at the University of Jammu, conceived to empower students with the freedom to craft their own academic journey. Breaking the boundaries of conventional education, DYD allows students to seamlessly blend domains from Technology and Economics to Psychology and Environmental Sciences creating a degree that is truly their own. Guided by distinguished faculty and enriched through hands-on experiences, industry exposure, and innovation challenges, the programme nurtures thinkers, creators, and leaders who are prepared not just for careers, but for life. At DYD, your curiosity is the curriculum.
