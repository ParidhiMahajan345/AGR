FROM THE DESK OF THE PRO CHANCELLOR

CHIEF MINISTER

JAMMU & KASHMIR

Dear Students,

I am pleased to write this message for the Handbook of the Design Your Degree (DYD) Program 2025, an innovative initiative by the University of Jammu. This forward-thinking program is focused to empower the students by way of creating customized courses aligning with their interest and make them future ready by way of access to a wide range of resources and opportunities, including internships, research projects and extra-curricular activities.

The program is designed to provide students with the freedom to break away from traditional academic constraints and cultivate a well rounded, personalized education. By offering such flexibility and interdisciplinary learning, the University of Jammu empowers its students to take ownership of their learning, fostering independent thought and intellectual curiosity.

As Pro-Chancellor, I commend the University of Jammu for its commitment to educational innovation. The DYD Program is a significant step toward nurturing independent thinkers and future leaders who can contribute meaningfully to society.

I extend my best wishes to the leadership team, faculty members and students, enrolled in the DYD program, of the University of Jammu for the 'Handbook of the Design Your Degree Program (DYD) 2025. I trust that this Handbook will be an invaluable resource for both students and faculty as they embark on this exciting academic journey.

Jb. Omar Abdullah

Pro-Chancellor
