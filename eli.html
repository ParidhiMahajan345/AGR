Admission Procedure

Admission shall be made on the basis of an entrance test conducted by the University of Jammu followed by Group Discussion and Interview.

The eligible candidates shall be selected based on the following components:

S.No Component Weightage (%) Remarks
1 Academic 12th 10 Pro rata basis.
2 Entrance Test 60 General awareness & aptitude.
3 Group Discussion 15 Group-based activity.
4 Personal Interview 15 Individual interaction.

Candidates shall be called for Group Discussion and Personal Interview in the ratio of 1:3 based on combined score.

Final merit shall be calculated on the composite score of all components.
