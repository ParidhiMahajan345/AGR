University of
Jammu.

A Place of
Learning.

Located near the Tawi river and near the Trikuta hills, Jammu is widely known as the city of temples. Among its key landmarks, the University of Jammu stands out as a center of study and growth.

The University of Jammu holds an 'A++' Grade accreditation from NAAC. It was established in 1969 to offer higher learning, research, and technical education to students from all backgrounds.

The main goal of the university is to share knowledge, support new research ideas, and help students develop clear thinking and practical skills for the future.

Main Objectives

  • Provide high quality teaching and support research.
  • Help students gain practical skill and confidence.
  • Support regional culture and diversity.
  • Use simple technology to make learning easier.

Campus Highlights

No Vehicle Zone

Green Campus

Open green lawns, shade trees, and gardens offer a fresh space for daily study and group discussions.

Smart Central Library Access

24/7 digital resource access, e-journals, and quiet study zones with high-speed internet.

Smart Classrooms
